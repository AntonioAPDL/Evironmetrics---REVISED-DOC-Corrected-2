# Current manuscript structure and forecasting emphasis

The November 2026 comment-1 revision uses six main sections: introduction;
forecasting setting and data; Bayesian correction and predictive synthesis;
application specification and evaluation; results; discussion and conclusions.
The stable labels are `sec:introduction`, `sec:data`, `sec:methodology`,
`sec:evaluation`, `sec:forecastvalidation`, and `sec5`.

Data roles are established before the model. Scoring methods and comparison
variants precede results. The illustrative predictive synthesis precedes the
supporting historical interpretation. Full matrix construction and augmentation
are in appendices; all existing numerical results remain authoritative.

`scripts/manuscript_text_contract.py` checks active LaTeX labels and scientific
concepts without requiring a specific sentence. Comments cannot satisfy the
checks. Quantitative provenance, source selection, table values, and forecast
horizons are checked separately by the numerical/asset validators. Automated
checks do not establish prose quality; the compiled manuscript requires a
complete reading pass.

The earlier response to reviewers is retained as a historical document. Its
exact text requirements are independent of the current article's wording.

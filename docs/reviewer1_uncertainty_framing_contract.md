# Uncertainty terminology in the current article

The introduction distinguishes hydrological uncertainty (structure, parameters,
states, observations) from meteorological uncertainty (precipitation and related
forcing). The data section identifies the USGS discharge target, retrospective
products, forecast products, and precipitation/soil-moisture/climate covariates.
Source descriptions cite PRISM and ERA5-Land beside their application roles.

The article distinguishes posterior credible intervals for fitted quantile or
state summaries from pointwise central 95% predictive intervals for synthesized
future discharge. A predictive mean is described on the modeled log1p scale.

Workflow overview and uncertainty checks use active LaTeX and concept checks,
not exact sentences. Numerical manifests and source selections retain their
separate checks. Prior-round response text is preserved as history.

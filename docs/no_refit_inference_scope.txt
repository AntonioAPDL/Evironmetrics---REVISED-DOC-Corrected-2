Inference exposition and evidence scope

All reported application fits use mean-field variational Bayes with Gaussian
state recursions and channel-specific Laplace--Delta scale/skewness factors.
The parameter intervals summarize these approximate variational marginals.
Coefficient centers are means at the historical endpoint; source scale and
skewness centers are medians. Their intervals differ from observation predictive
intervals. Existing optimization stopping evidence does not determine the
approximation's accuracy relative to a full posterior.

The appendix conditional sampling displays are scoped to the historical
observation model. The state path draw conditions on the augmentation, channel
parameters, and specified evolution covariances. Historical augmentation/scale
identities and conditional FFBS moments have been checked algebraically and on
fixed synthetic examples. This does not provide a validated full-model Markov
chain or an empirical VB/MCMC comparison. The application results are unchanged.

The target-scope audit outcome is FORMULATION_SCOPED_WITH_OPEN_STEPS. Extending
the conditional building blocks to a full historical-and-forecast posterior
requires all member-specific augmentations and their multiplicities, active
state transitions, a coherent evolution-covariance target, an implemented
proposal mechanism, and finite-chain diagnostics. These require a separately
identified posterior experiment before any accuracy comparison can be reported.

The member likelihood treats residual contributions as conditionally independent
given shared states and channel parameters. State uncertainty induces marginal
dependence. Member counts alone do not determine a fixed information ratio, and
a shared observation-scale parameterization does not imply constant predictive
variance over lead time. The descriptive diagnostics concern the current inputs
and predictions; they do not test an alternative likelihood.

The optional representative source-precision report is omitted with outcome
OPTIONAL_SKIPPED_SOURCE_CONTRACT. Existing saved pseudo-observation variances and
terminal moments can refer to different update phases. The source-label and
phase correspondence required for numerical interpretation is unresolved.
No unverified precision-share table or historical-error conclusion follows.
The source/member count explanation and raw-product error/spread diagnostics
remain independent of this optional omission.

Scope and authority

This additive revision uses the selected results at article commit
2583ee41c1367e65d4d71ed36cf280a4f555a513 and workflow commit
f69d4c8ac144f405b3535c4d8c8015cc8af9e2db. It does not fit a model, retrain a
reference, generate a posterior draw or replace a selected run. Original tables,
score values and figures remain protected by the point-2 manifest. Existing
point-1 presentation structure and the point-2 external comparisons remain.

The required additions are exact settings; correct coefficient estimands and
feature identities; approximate-uncertainty wording; explicit member-likelihood
and algorithm scope; and saved-output score, calibration and discharge-scale
verification. The workflow planning package retains stable N00-N16 task IDs.
The workflow execution record distinguishes delivered work from conditional
diagnostics that cannot be justified from current exports and experiments that
require new inference. No empty result placeholder is inserted in the paper.

The replay uses only bundled inputs, R and the versions recorded in
verification/session_info.txt. The R entrypoint lives with its helper and
configuration in reproduce/; absolute original paths in registries are
provenance only. Inputs are checksummed before use, executed sources are
checksummed before and after derivation, and output must be a fresh directory.
Compare result CSVs and the PNG with the bound release copies. Elapsed time and
the path-specific invocation record are expected to differ in a fresh run.

Reporting metadata has its own portable Python replay in reporting/README.txt.
Historical conditional identities have a separate deterministic verification
script and report in inference_scope/. That verification uses synthetic fixed
numbers and establishes conditional algebra, not MCMC convergence or VB accuracy.

The synthesis_diagnostics/ README gives a separate compact replay from the
snapshotted own-fit anchors and final delivered quantiles. The initial extraction
reads and hashes 30 existing predictive cubes; compact replay does not require
those full cubes. It reports role-specific check loss, hit counts, displacement
and crossings. It does not compute a pre-synthesis CRPS from seven anchors.

Case and score contracts

There are 14 methods, 70 method-origin representations, 140 observation cases
and 1,860 available forecast cases. Raw NWS contributes eight leads per origin;
the other methods contribute 28. Both scales are retained: 3,720 score rows,
26,040 targeted-quantile rows and 3,720 interval rows. Unique keys, dates,
finite member counts and complete denominators fail closed if inconsistent.
No member is silently discarded, duplicated or clipped.

The primary new score is exact CRPS of each saved equally weighted empirical
distribution, including deterministic baseline grids. Its skill denominator is
the same frozen climatology representation on matching cases and scale. The
old quantile-grid score is preserved separately and replayed, never renamed.
The iid pairwise correction is a sensitivity for stochastic samples, with an
explicit additional assumption for physical ensemble members. Deterministic
grids and point masses have a named not-applicable status; there is no adjusted
skill column. AL/exAL matrices have 2,000 draws, Gaussian matrices 64 or 48,
and raw products 51 or seven members. Repeating or thinning members is avoided.

The four HE4 principal competitors retain their delivered targeted quantiles.
Other new sample quantiles use type 8, raw/deterministic quantiles type 1, and
persistence its point value. Selected multivariate type-8 limits reproduce
their displayed exports. Original-scale means and CRPS transform every stored
value, whereas declared quantiles and limits are inverse-transformed from log.
Exact strict/weak/tie and closed-interval events are evaluated on the declared
log event scale and inherited by the inverse-scale records. This preserves a
true discrete tie even when separate floating-point exponentiation differs at
the last bit. No tolerance creates a tie.

Coverage includes lower, inside and upper counts, width and proper interval
score. Lead-specific raw-product errors/spreads are descriptive, with only five
origins per lead. No independent-day significance claim is made. Stored support
is reported in support_audit.csv, including negative transformed values and
extreme tails; no unrecorded support correction is applied.

Important verified findings

Selected exAL-M-T1 exact-ECDF log CRPS is 0.2015622217 over 28 days and
0.2991900353 over eight days, with skill 0.6860234114 and 0.5896529510.
Its central 95% intervals cover 123/140 and 29/40 cases. The two December
origins account for the short-horizon misses. On the discharge scale, selected
28-day CRPS is 6.2688515943; at eight days raw NWS scores 6.63303 versus selected
7.49362. These are separate criteria and the reversal is retained explicitly.

Coefficient metadata preserves all 27 available endpoint-summary strings,
identifies all 12 states/features, and does not invent unexported states 32-34.
The main base-feature rows are unchanged; displayed loadings are conditional
terminal state coefficients, not physical causal effects or historical averages.
All parameter and historical-state bands are described as approximate VB
uncertainty, separately from observation predictive intervals.

Limits retained in the evidence

The diagnostic-only historical covariance-index extraction is repaired in the
workflow with a time-varying covariance regression case. Optional regeneration
of late-window coefficient bands is skipped because the required covariances
are absent in the listed selected exports. Optional precision-share reporting
is skipped because the source/phase contract is incomplete. No numerical
precision shares or unsupported band replacements enter the manuscript.

Historical conditional-sampling displays are explicitly bounded and their
identities checked; they are not advertised as a tested full-model sampler.
A full VB/MCMC posterior comparison, alternative member-dependence/lead-scale
models and further fitting/convergence experiments require later inference.

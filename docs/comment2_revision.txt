External-reference specification
CLIM-SEASONAL uses the gauge history from 1987-05-29 through each origin,
with +/-15 month/day positions on a circular leap-year-2000 calendar.
PERSISTENCE repeats the last observed log1p discharge as a point mass.
EMOS-GLOFAS uses a normal distribution truncated below zero on log1p discharge,
with pre-truncation location a+b*ensemble_mean and variance
c+d*unbiased_ensemble_variance, calculated from the 51 transformed members.
Fits are separate by lead and use up to 365 preceding issue days, issue<origin
and target<=origin, with at least 100 version-compatible cases. Four deterministic
L-BFGS-B starts minimize analytic truncated-normal training CRPS, retaining the
best converged result. Numerical bounds and scaling are recorded with each
fit. During training-only conditioning checks, the initial artificial d=100
upper bound was active; the baseline worker expanded this bound and scaled
that parameter before the accepted run. The final generated baseline manifest
is the authority for these numerical settings.
The EMOS version-family change used for training is 2021-05-26. These are
application specifications; the cited papers motivate the model family, not
this particular training window or numerical bounds.

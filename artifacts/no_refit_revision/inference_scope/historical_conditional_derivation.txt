Conditional identities for the historical observation model

Scope

Fix the target quantile, the historical observations, the state-transition and
evolution covariance sequence, the initial-state prior, and the hyperparameters.
The identities below condition on that covariance sequence. They do not supply
a transition for uncertain evolution covariances or a complete Markov chain for
the joint historical and forecast target. No posterior samples are produced by
this derivation or its deterministic checks.

Use the augmentation in the manuscript, for one channel j and historical time t:

  y_t = mu_t + A*v_t + C*sigma*abs(gamma)*s_t + e_t,
  e_t | v_t,sigma,gamma ~ Normal(0, B*sigma*v_t),
  v_t | sigma ~ Exponential(mean=sigma),
  s_t ~ Normal(0,1) truncated to s_t > 0.

Here mu_t = h_t' eta_t; A,B,C are functions of the current gamma and target
quantile. The scale prior is IG(a_sigma,b_sigma), with density proportional to
sigma^(-a_sigma-1)*exp(-b_sigma/sigma). The gamma prior is the stated truncated
Student-t density over the admissible target-quantile bounds.

GIG convention

  GIG(p,a,b) has density proportional to
  x^(p-1)*exp[-(a*x + b/x)/2], x > 0.

The v conditional

Keeping the other quantities fixed and writing r_t = y_t - mu_t, its log kernel
is the sum of -log(v_t)/2, the Gaussian squared residual term, and -v_t/sigma.
Collecting terms gives:

  p_v = 1/2,
  a_v = (A^2/B + 2)/sigma,
  b_v = [r_t - C*sigma*abs(gamma)*s_t]^2/(B*sigma).

The s conditional

The positive-truncated normal prior supplies -s_t^2/2. Combining it with the
Gaussian residual gives the underlying normal variance and mean:

  ell_t = [1 + C^2*gamma^2*sigma/(B*v_t)]^(-1),
  m_t = ell_t*C*abs(gamma)*(r_t-A*v_t)/(B*v_t).

The second parameter is variance, not standard deviation. The truncation remains
s_t > 0; its normalizing constant is fixed when deriving this full conditional.

The sigma conditional

Let n_j be the number of observations in the channel's stated target and define
d_t = y_t - mu_t - A*v_t. The Gaussian likelihood and exponential augmentation
density jointly contribute sigma^(-3*n_j/2). After including its prior, the
conditional is GIG(p_sigma,a_sigma_star,b_sigma_star), where:

  p_sigma = -a_sigma - 3*n_j/2,
  a_sigma_star = sum_t [C*abs(gamma)*s_t]^2/(B*v_t),
  b_sigma_star = sum_t d_t^2/(B*v_t) + 2*sum_t v_t + 2*b_sigma.

For the historical-only construction, n_j = T under complete channel support.
If a_sigma_star=0, the proper inverse-gamma limit has shape -p_sigma and scale
b_sigma_star/2. In particular this limit applies when gamma=0.

For the full external-source likelihood, the same algebra sums over every
included historical observation and every issued member/lead. With complete
historical support, the count becomes T + I_j*K_j. This algebra identifies the
required multiplicity; it does not implement the other steps of a full sampler.

The gamma conditional target

At every proposed gamma, A(gamma), B(gamma), and C(gamma) must be recomputed.
Ignoring constants independent of gamma, the historical log conditional is:

  log prior_gamma(gamma) - T*log(B(gamma))/2
  - sum_t [y_t-mu_t-A(gamma)*v_t
           -C(gamma)*sigma*abs(gamma)*s_t]^2/v_t
      /[2*B(gamma)*sigma].

If a symmetric random walk is made on xi with
gamma = L + (U-L)*logistic(xi), the transformed target adds the Jacobian
log(U-L) + log(logistic(xi)) + log(1-logistic(xi)). A generic instruction to use
MH leaves proposal tuning, initialization, finite-run diagnostics, and empirical
accuracy unspecified. This bundle establishes no sampler implementation.

The historical state conditional

Conditioned on the augmentation, channel parameters and specified evolution
covariances, the historical state trajectory is Gaussian. The filter uses
b_t = C*sigma*abs(gamma)*s_t + A*v_t componentwise and diagonal observation
covariance O_t = Diag(B*sigma*v_t). Initialize m_0,C_0 at their specified prior.
Use the manuscript's forward moments a_t,R_t,m_t,C_t. The backward draw is:

  eta_T | historical data, remaining quantities ~ Normal(m_T,C_T),
  eta_t | eta_(t+1), historical data, remaining quantities
    ~ Normal(m_t + C_t*D_(t+1)'*R_(t+1)^(-1)*(eta_(t+1)-a_(t+1)),
             C_t-C_t*D_(t+1)'*R_(t+1)^(-1)*D_(t+1)*C_t).

This is the conditional distribution given the sampled next state. Its covariance
differs from the marginal backward-smoothing covariance used by VB. Explicitly
stating this conditioning corrects the meaning of the display without changing
the numerical formula.

Limits of the historical identities

The original historical FFBS display ends at T, and its scale sums count T
observations. An isolated forecast-v augmentation loop supplies neither the
forecast s updates nor the stacked forecast state/parameter updates. Scoping the
display to the historical model and removing that isolated loop yields a coherent
set of conditional identities. It does not claim a full-model MCMC algorithm.
The treatment of uncertain evolution covariances, all member-specific forecast
augmentations, dynamic active-state dimensions, proposal implementation, and
chain diagnostics remain requirements for a later matching-target experiment.

Deterministic verification

Run verify_conditional_identities.py to check the v, s, sigma and gamma log-kernel
identities over fixed legal inputs, including the gamma=0 scale limit. It also
compares the FFBS terminal and backward conditional moments with direct Gaussian
conditioning in a three-time, two-state example. These are algebra checks using
synthetic fixed numbers; no random values, article observations, fitted objects,
or Markov chain are used. conditional_identity_verification.json records the
actual checks and numerical errors. Passing them does not assess VB accuracy,
convergence of any empirical fit, or correctness of an executable full sampler.
